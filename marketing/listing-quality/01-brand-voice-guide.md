# Wise Hands — listing brand voice guide (DRAFT v1)

Draft only. Nothing here posts anything. Audience: Nauru buyers. Language: plain English for the page; Roman Urdu only in internal Owner notes.

## The voice in one line
**Short, calm, premium, honest.** A good tool, described as it really is, at a fair price, from someone local.

## Do
- Lead with the item: brand + model + one plain-fact hook ("Compact, ready for the job.") — a hook is allowed only if every word is true of that item.
- Say the condition first and plainly: *Used. Visible marks and wear, shown in the photos.*
- Say exactly what is included, and what is not ("Battery and charger not included").
- Put the price in AUD, once, clearly: `AUD 350`.
- End with one clear Nauru action: *Message us to arrange pickup or delivery in Nauru.*
- Keep it short: 4–6 lines for a post, 3 lines for a story card.
- Use "Just arrived", "Available now", "Used, as pictured".

## Never say (hard rules)
1. **"Tested" / "working" / "fully functional"** unless the Owner tested that exact item and says so in writing. Source it: `[OWNER: tested by ____ on ____]`.
   - Only exception: a supplier's test statement may be repeated as *"Seller-tested before purchase (supplier's statement; not re-tested by us)"*, and only when the Owner accepts it. Never shorten it to "tested" or "working".
2. **Luxury or maker-prestige claims** ("genuine", "authentic", "original", "professional-grade", "premium brand") **without proof** (receipt, serial plate, photo). Brand name goes in only as printed on the item itself.
3. **Warranty / guarantee / returns promises.** No "money back", no "guaranteed". If asked, the Owner answers by hand.
4. **Invented specs** (power, size, speed, age, battery life). Only what is on the label in the photo or in the Owner's note.
5. **Pressure and fake scarcity** ("last one!", "hurry", "everyone wants this") unless literally true (quantity 1 may be said as "one available").
6. **Discount games** ("was AUD 500"). One price, the Owner's final price.
7. **Comparisons or health/safety claims.**

## Elegant, not loud
- No ALL CAPS, no more than one emoji, no exclamation chains.
- No slang that excludes. Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Photos carry the story; the words only confirm it.

## Sourcing rule (every fact has a source)
| Fact | Allowed source |
|---|---|
| Brand / model | Printed on the item (photo) or catalog entry |
| Condition, marks | Photos + Owner note |
| Included / not included | Photos + Owner note |
| Tested / working | Owner's own written confirmation only |
| Price | Owner's final, AUD |

If a fact has no source, delete the line. A shorter honest post beats a longer one.

## Review before posting (Owner, 60 seconds)
- [ ] Every `[OWNER: ...]` filled or its line deleted
- [ ] Nothing in the "Never say" list
- [ ] Price matches the Owner's final price
- [ ] Photos match the words
