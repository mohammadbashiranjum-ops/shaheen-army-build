# Photo shot list — CC-0022, CC-0024, CC-0025, CC-0034 (DRAFT)

**DRAFT ONLY. Nothing is posted, uploaded or sent.** These are the real photos of **our own unit** that must be taken (or re-taken) before any listing. Source/supplier photos are not used unless the Owner approves them and they pass the privacy check.

## Photo spec (all items)
- **JPEG**, **4:5 portrait** (e.g. 1080 × 1350 px), **at least 1080 px wide**. Shoot larger and crop to 4:5; keep the item inside the centre so a crop never cuts it.
- **The first image sets the carousel ratio** — make the hero 4:5 and keep every other image 4:5.
- **3 to 6 photos per item** (minimum 3 approved before the item is postable).
- **Real photos of the actual unit.** No stock images, no edits that hide marks or wear, no filters.

## Background and light
- Plain, clean, neutral surface (a table or floor with a plain sheet); no clutter, no other items.
- Soft daylight from a window or open shade; avoid harsh sun and hard shadows. No flash glare on metal or plastic.
- Same background and light across all photos of one item.

## Privacy — must not appear
- **No people** (faces, hands with ID, reflections of people), **no IDs, receipts, parcel labels, addresses, phone numbers**, no personal documents.
- **No serial numbers** visible: cover or crop them out. Show the **brand and model** only.
- Check the background for anything personal before approving. Mark each photo `privacy-checked: yes/no` in the approval list.

## Standard order (adjust per item)
1. **Hero** — whole item, 3/4 angle, centred, fills about 70% of the frame.
2. **Included** — everything that comes with it, laid out together (and nothing that is not included).
3. **Model label** — brand and model readable, serial covered or cropped.
4. **Condition** — honest close-up of marks, scuffs or wear. Shown, not hidden.
5. **Second angle / in-hand scale** (optional, hands only if no ID/ring/identity detail; otherwise skip).
6. **Price card** (optional) — added later by the Owner, not a photo.
