# Caption templates — Facebook, Instagram, story, carousel (DRAFT v1)

Draft only. Replace `[OWNER: ...]` with the Owner's confirmed facts, or delete the line. Do not post from this file.
Currency is always **AUD**. Price is the Owner's final.

## A. Facebook post (4–6 lines)
> {Brand Model} — just arrived.
> Condition: [OWNER: used/new + marks or wear, as shown in the photos]
> Included: [OWNER: items shown in photos]. Not included: [OWNER: what is missing, e.g. battery, charger, case]
> Price: AUD [OWNER: final price]
> Message us to arrange pickup or delivery in Nauru.
> #Nauru #UsedTools #PowerTools #WiseHands

Structure: hook line → condition → included/excluded → price → CTA → 3–5 hashtags.
Optional line (only if the Owner tested it): `> [OWNER: Tested by us on {date} and working.]`

## B. Instagram caption (shorter, one idea per line)
> {Brand Model}.
> [OWNER: condition, one short line]
> Included: [OWNER: ...] · Not included: [OWNER: ...]
> AUD [OWNER: final price]
> DM us to arrange pickup or delivery in Nauru.
> .
> #Nauru #NauruMarketplace #UsedTools #PowerTools #WiseHands

(The lone `.` line keeps hashtags visually apart; hashtags stay at 3–5.)

## C. Story card (3 lines, one card per item)
> Just arrived: {Brand Model}
> AUD [OWNER: final price]
> DM to reserve

Rules: one item per card; the item photo is the background; no extra text; no sticker links until the post is visible on the account (read back).

## D. Carousel order rules (Instagram/Facebook, 3–6 photos)
1. **Slide 1 — hero:** the whole item, clean background, best light.
2. **Slide 2 — included:** everything that comes with it, laid out together.
3. **Slide 3 — label/model plate:** readable brand/model/serial (proves the brand claim).
4. **Slide 4 — condition:** honest close-up of marks and wear (shown, not hidden).
5. **Slide 5 — scale/in-hand** (optional): shows size.
6. **Slide 6 — price card** (optional): `AUD {price} · Nauru pickup/delivery`.
Never reorder to hide wear. Photos must match the caption.

## E. Hashtag rule
3–5 per post: `#Nauru` always; one category (`#PowerTools` / `#HandTools`); `#UsedTools` for used items; `#WiseHands`. No brand hashtags unless the brand is printed on the item. No more than 5.

## F. Quick check
- Hook is true of this item? · Condition stated first? · Included **and** not included? · AUD price once? · Nauru CTA? · 3–5 hashtags? · No "tested/working/warranty/genuine" without proof?
